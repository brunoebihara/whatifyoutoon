What if it happened to you?

Cartoon stories, real facts. A serialized short-video series that puts you in the story, like "What if you won tonight's Powerball?", told part by part with real rules and numbers and hypothetical people.

Watch

New parts go up on YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok as @whatifyoutoon. Start with the series playlist on YouTube: What if you won tonight's Powerball?

The whatifyou publisher

"whatifyou publisher" is a small private tool the channel owner runs on their own computer to upload the channel's own videos (title, description, thumbnail, playlist) to the channel's own YouTube and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account or data.
