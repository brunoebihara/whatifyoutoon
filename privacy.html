Privacy Policy

whatifyou publisher · last updated 4 November 2026

"whatifyou publisher" (the "App") is a private tool used only by the owner of the @whatifyoutoon channel to publish the channel's own videos to the channel's own accounts. It has no other users.

What the App accesses

  • YouTube (through the YouTube API Services): permission to upload videos to the owner's channel, set their title, description, tags and thumbnail, and add them to the owner's playlists.
  • Instagram (through the Instagram API): permission to publish Reels to the owner's professional account.

The App does not read, collect or store any information about viewers or other people, and does not access any account other than the owner's.

How data is stored

The sign-in tokens Google and Instagram issue are stored only on the owner's own computer, in a private folder readable only by the owner's user account. The App has no server and no database, and sends data only to the platforms it publishes to (Google/YouTube, Instagram) and, for Instagram uploads, to the owner's private file storage, where each video is kept for at most two hours.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or any purpose other than publishing the owner's videos.

Google and YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings; the owner can also delete the stored tokens from their computer.

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
